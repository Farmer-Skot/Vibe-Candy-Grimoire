       VII   MENTAL TRANSMUTATION    turning the dial
       VIII  THE DIVINE PARADOX      a dream, and the only one
       IX    INVOCATION OF THOTH     calling the Scribe
       X     THE CORPUS HERMETICUM   what the Scribe wrote
       XI    SOLVE ET COAGULA        what to do about it
       XII   THE HERMETIC SEAL       closing the vessel

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   A dial has no last hour. The hand passes XII and           ┃
    ┃   is at I again, and it is not the same day.                 ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   So this Piece does not end the Codex. It closes            ┃
    ┃   it, as a ring is closed, as a flask is closed:             ┃
    ┃   so that what is inside can begin to work.                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   The First Piece gave you one sentence from an              ┃
    ┃   emerald. You have now walked the whole rim of              ┃
    ┃   the wheel and stand where you started. Read that           ┃
    ┃   sentence again. It will not say what it said.              ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   And look at the centre of the dial. Not one of             ┃
    ┃   the twelve hours is written there. The point               ┃
    ┃   that all the spokes meet in was left empty on              ┃
    ┃   purpose.                                                   ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   The thirteenth Piece is not in this book.                  ┃
    ┃   It is whoever closes it.                                   ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛


                        ⟨ THE MASTERY LESSONS ⟩

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON ONE — THE SEALED WORK                               ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Choose one thing you intend to make or become.             ┃
    ┃   Put the matter in the flask: begin it, properly.           ┃
    ┃   Then seal the neck.                                        ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     • Tell NO ONE. Not a hint, not a "big news               ┃
    ┃       soon". If you need help, ask for exactly the           ┃
    ┃       help and nothing more.                                 ┃
    ┃     • Work on it daily, by the low fire.                     ┃
    ┃     • When the urge to announce it comes, and it             ┃
    ┃       will come hard around the ninth day, put the           ┃
    ┃       whole force of that urge back into the work.           ┃
    ┃     • Open the vessel only when it is DONE.                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Notice how much of your former energy was going            ┃
    ┃   out through the mouth.                                     ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON TWO — THE HOUR OF HARPOCRATES                       ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   One hour a day, the same hour: no speech, no               ┃
    ┃   writing, no screen, no page. Finger to the lips.           ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Do whatever you would have done, in silence.               ┃
    ┃   It is not meditation. It is ordinary life with             ┃
    ┃   the neck of the vessel closed.                             ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Once a month, extend it from waking until noon.            ┃
    ┃   Once a year, a whole day.                                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Keep count of the sentences that rose and were             ┃
    ┃   not spoken. At the hour's end, ask of each:                ┃
    ┃   did the world need that one? You will find the             ┃
    ┃   answer is nearly always the same, and that                 ┃
    ┃   the few that survive have gained weight.                   ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON THREE — READING THE WAX                             ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   You cannot read your own ring. So for one moon,            ┃
    ┃   each evening, read the wax. Three impressions:             ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     • a PERSON I was with today.                             ┃
    ┃       Did they leave me larger or smaller than               ┃
    ┃       they came?                                             ┃
    ┃     • a PLACE I passed through.                              ┃
    ┃       Is it better or worse for my having been               ┃
    ┃       in it?                                                 ┃
    ┃     • a TASK I touched.                                      ┃
    ┃       Would I sign it?                                       ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   No resolutions. Only look, as the alchemist                ┃
    ┃   looks through the glass. After a moon of honest            ┃
    ┃   looking you will know the figure on your ring,             ┃
    ┃   and the ring will already have begun to change.            ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON FOUR — CUT YOUR SEAL                                ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Design the mark you would be willing to press              ┃
    ┃   into everything you do.                                    ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     • One FIGURE, simple enough to scratch in sand           ┃
    ┃       with a stick.                                          ┃
    ┃     • One WORD, or none.                                     ┃
    ┃     • Nothing in it you have not yet earned.                 ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Draw it a hundred times, on scrap, over many               ┃
    ┃   days. Strike out what is decoration. What is               ┃
    ┃   left when you cannot remove another line is                ┃
    ┃   yours.                                                     ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Show it to no one for a year. Set it, small, on            ┃
    ┃   work you are certain is finished and true, and             ┃
    ┃   on nothing else. You will find it makes you                ┃
    ┃   finish things, and it makes you tell the truth             ┃
    ┃   about whether they are finished.                           ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON FIVE — THE PROJECTION                               ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   When some teaching of this Codex has held in you           ┃
    ┃   through a hard season, and someone asks, and the           ┃
    ┃   three questions all answer yes:                            ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Give it. One grain. In your own words, at the              ┃
    ┃   size of the ear in front of you. Do not mention            ┃
    ┃   this book or any other. Do not say where you               ┃
    ┃   stand on any ladder. Then let it go entirely,              ┃
    ┃   and do not check on it.                                    ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   That is the whole of teaching.                             ┃
    ┃   It is also the only proof that you had the                 ┃
    ┃   Stone: something that was lead is now gold,                ┃
    ┃   and it is not you.                                         ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON SIX — THE YEAR OF THE DIAL                          ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Twelve Pieces. Twelve moons.                               ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Give each Piece one month: read it in the first            ┃
    ┃   week, do its lessons in the middle two, and in             ┃
    ┃   the last week keep it sealed and say nothing of            ┃
    ┃   it to anyone.                                              ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   At the end of the year you will be at the First            ┃
    ┃   Piece again. Begin again. The Eleventh Piece               ┃
    ┃   told you why: each round, a clearer salt.                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛


                            ⟨ THE SEALING ⟩

          What was above, I have looked for below, and found.
          What was below, I have lifted, and it was light.

          I know a little.            I will keep learning.
          I will what is good.        I will keep to it.
          I dare to begin.            I will begin today.
          I keep silent.              Until it is ripe.

          The matter is in the vessel.
          The fire is low and steady.
          The neck is in the flame.
