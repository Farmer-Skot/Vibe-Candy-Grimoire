    │   The formula is not performed once.                         │
    │                                                              │
    │   The old recipes say to dissolve and coagulate the          │
    │   matter seven times, or ten, or "a thousand". What          │
    │   they mean is: until. Each crystal is purer than            │
    │   the last, and each is dissolved in its turn.               │
    │                                                              │
    │   This is the answer to a discouragement every               │
    │   worker meets: "I thought I had dealt with this."           │
    │   You had. That crystal was real. Now it is going            │
    │   back into the water, and what comes out will be            │
    │   clearer by one more degree.                                │
    │                                                              │
    │   And it is the answer to the opposite error, the            │
    │   one who, having had one great dissolving, speaks           │
    │   of it for forty years. One round makes a                   │
    │   cleaner salt. It does not make the Stone.                  │
    │                                                              │

    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   On any plane, at any time, one of the two is               ┃
    ┃   being asked of you. The whole skill is knowing             ┃
    ┃   which.                                                     ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     If you are STUCK, bored, defended, brittle,              ┃
    ┃     repeating yourself, sure of everything:                  ┃
    ┃                                       ──► SOLVE.             ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     If you are SCATTERED, drifting, starting and             ┃
    ┃     never finishing, open to everything:                     ┃
    ┃                                       ──► COAGULA.           ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   And know your own bias. Everyone has a favourite           ┃
    ┃   hand, and reaches for it even when the Work needs          ┃
    ┃   the other. The dissolver, in crisis, dissolves             ┃
    ┃   more. The fixer, in crisis, clamps down. The               ┃
    ┃   medicine is nearly always the operation you                ┃
    ┃   like less.                                                 ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃

                        ⟨ THE MASTERY LESSONS ⟩

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON ONE — THE KITCHEN WORK                              ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Do it with your hands before your head.                    ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     1. Take a spoonful of coarse, grey or dirty salt.        ┃
    ┃        Look at it closely. Draw it.                          ┃
    ┃     2. Stir it into a little warm water until no             ┃
    ┃        grain is left. Watch it vanish.                       ┃
    ┃     3. Pour it through a paper filter or clean               ┃
    ┃        cloth into a shallow dish. Keep what the              ┃
    ┃        filter caught. Look at that too.                      ┃
    ┃     4. Set the dish somewhere warm and still.                ┃
    ┃        Cover it loosely against dust. Do not stir.           ┃
    ┃     5. Look once a day. Do nothing else.                     ┃
    ┃     6. When the water is gone, look at what is               ┃
    ┃        there. Draw it beside your first drawing.             ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   It takes about a week and costs nothing. You               ┃
    ┃   will understand this Piece better from that dish           ┃
    ┃   than from rereading it ten times.                          ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON TWO — THE SOLVE OF A BELIEF                         ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Choose one fixed belief about yourself that                ┃
    ┃   begins "I am the kind of person who..." or                 ┃
    ┃   "I could never...".                                        ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Write it at the top of a page. Then loosen it:             ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     • When did I first hold this? Who gave it to me?         ┃
    ┃     • What is the evidence for it? Against it?               ┃
    ┃     • Who would I be for one day without it?                 ┃
    ┃     • What does holding it protect me from?                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Do not replace it. That is the discipline. Leave           ┃
    ┃   the page as a set of open questions and live               ┃
    ┃   inside them for seven days. Notice the urge to             ┃
    ┃   conclude, and decline it.                                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   On the eighth day, write what is now true.                 ┃
    ┃   It will be shorter and you will not need to                ┃
    ┃   defend it.                                                 ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON THREE — THE COAGULA OF A VOW                        ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Choose one thing you have long "been meaning to"           ┃
    ┃   embody: a vapour that has hung over your life              ┃
    ┃   for years without ever raining.                            ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Fix it:                                                    ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     • ONE act. Not a programme.                              ┃
    ┃     • SMALL enough to do on your worst day.                  ┃
    ┃     • At a named TIME and PLACE.                             ┃
    ┃     • Told to ONE other person.                              ┃
    ┃     • For FORTY days, unbroken. If broken, the               ┃
    ┃       count returns to one, without drama.                   ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   The vapour will resist. It preferred being a               ┃
    ┃   possibility; possibilities are never wrong. On             ┃
    ┃   the fortieth day you will have less potential              ┃
    ┃   and more reality. That is the trade, and it is a           ┃
    ┃   good one.                                                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON FOUR — THE DAILY ROUND                              ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   THE BREATH, any time, twenty-one rounds:                   ┃
    ┃       out  ·  ·  ·   silently: "solve"                       ┃
    ┃                      let the shoulders, the jaw,             ┃
    ┃                      and one thought go                      ┃
    ┃       in   ·  ·  ·   silently: "coagula"                     ┃
    ┃                      gather, lengthen the spine,             ┃
    ┃                      arrive in the body                      ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   THE NIGHT, lying down:                                     ┃
    ┃       Go back through the day in reverse. At each            ┃
    ┃       scene: "This is done. I release it." Do not            ┃
    ┃       judge it; the Scribe has it. Let the day               ┃
    ┃       dissolve before you do.                                ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   THE MORNING, before rising:                                ┃
    ┃       One sentence only. "Today I give form to               ┃
    ┃       ________." Then stand up into it.                      ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   A life that does this small round daily will not           ┃
    ┃   often need the great dissolvings forced upon it.           ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛

    ┏━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┓
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   LESSON FIVE — THE AUDIT OF THE TWO HANDS                   ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   At each turning of the season, draw two columns.           ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃     TOO FIXED                 TOO FLUID                      ┃
    ┃     ───────────────────       ───────────────────            ┃
    ┃     what in my life has       what in my life has            ┃
    ┃     gone hard, automatic,     no edges, no hours,            ┃
    ┃     unexamined, joyless?      no commitments, no             ┃
    ┃                               floor?                         ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Choose ONE from each column.                               ┃
    ┃   To the first, apply Solve: question it, loosen             ┃
    ┃   it, take a season off from it.                             ┃
    ┃   To the second, apply Coagula: give it a time, a            ┃
    ┃   place, a limit, a promise.                                 ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┃   Then write at the foot of the page which column            ┃
    ┃   was easier to fill. That is your favourite hand.           ┃
    ┃   Watch it.                                                  ┃
    ┃                                                              ┃
    ┗━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━┛
