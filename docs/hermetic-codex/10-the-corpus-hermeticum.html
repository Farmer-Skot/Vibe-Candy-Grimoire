    c. 100–300   ●  Written in Greek in Roman Egypt, by unknown
                 │  hands, in the name of Hermes-Thoth.
                 │
    c. 1050      ●  A Byzantine scholar is reading a collected
                 │  copy. The West has forgotten all but the
                 │  Latin Asclepius.
                 │
    1460         ●  A monk brings a Greek manuscript to Florence.
                 │  Cosimo de' Medici, old and near death, tells
                 │  Marsilio Ficino to set Plato aside and
                 │  translate Hermes FIRST.
                 │
    1463–1471    ●  Ficino finishes; the book is printed. Europe
                 │  believes it is reading a sage as old as Moses.
                 │  The Renaissance drinks it like water.
                 │
    1614         ●  Isaac Casaubon shows from the Greek itself
                 │  that the texts belong to the first centuries
                 │  of our era, not to the age of the pyramids.
                 │
    1945         ●  A sealed jar is dug up near Nag Hammadi in
                 │  Egypt. Among its books: Hermetic treatises
                 │  in Coptic, unknown until that day.
                 │
    now          ◉  You.

                   ⟨ III · POIMANDRES · THE VISION ⟩

                               Treatise I

      Once, when thought came to me of the things that are,
      and my mind soared, and my body's senses were held down
      as in the heavy sleep after too much food or toil,

      there seemed to come to me a Being of vast and
      boundless size, who called my name and said:

             "What do you wish to hear and see,
              and to learn and know by thought?"

      "Who are you?" I said.

             "I am Poimandres, the Mind of the Sovereign Power.
              I know what you wish, and I am with you everywhere."

     "That Light is I, Mind, your God,
      who was before the wet nature that appeared out of darkness.
      And the shining Word that came from Mind is the Son of God."

     "How so?" I said.

     "Know it thus: that in YOU which sees and hears
      is the Word of the Lord, and your Mind is God the Father.
      They are not divided from one another.
      Their union is Life."

            God gave Reason to all, but not Mind.
            Not from any grudging; Mind was set in the midst
            as a prize for souls to win.

            He filled a great bowl with it and sent it down,
            and appointed a herald to cry to human hearts:

                    "IMMERSE YOURSELF IN THIS BOWL,
                     you who are able;
                     you who trust you will rise
                     to the One who sent it down;
                     you who know WHY YOU CAME TO BE."

            Those who heard and plunged in received gnosis
            and became complete. Those who missed the cry
            have reason but not Mind. They do not know
            for what they were made, or by whom.

                    ⟨ VII · THE CRY IN THE STREET ⟩

                          Treatises I and VII

        O people, earth-born,
        who have given yourselves up to drunkenness and sleep
        and to ignorance of God:

                BE SOBER.

        Where are you rushing, you drunkards,
        who have swallowed the neat wine of unknowing
        and cannot hold it, and already are sick with it?

        Stop. Stand still. Look up with the eyes of the heart;
        if not all of you, then those who can.

        Do not be swept down by the great flood.
        Use the back-current, you who are able,
        make for the harbour of safety,
        and find one to take you by the hand
        and lead you to the doors of knowing,
        where the bright light is, pure of darkness,
        where no one is drunk, but all are sober,
        gazing with the heart on the One who wishes to be seen.

              divinity. You said no one can be saved before
              being born again. I do not know from what womb
              a human is born a second time, or from what seed.

              The seed is the true Good.

     HERMES   The Will of God.

     HERMES   What can I say? This thing is not taught.

    ┃                                                              ┃

    ┃            the mask of love                                  ┃
    ┃     SUN ☉  my need to be the one in charge                   ┃

           every soul and heart stretched out to you,
           O Name untroubled, honoured with the name of God
           and praised with the name of Father.

           To everyone and everything you have shown
           a father's kindness, and affection, and love.

           You have given us mind, and speech, and knowledge:
           mind, that we may understand you;
           speech, that we may call upon you;
           knowledge, that we may know you.

           We rejoice, for you have shown us yourself.
           We rejoice, for while we are yet in the body
           you have made us divine through your knowledge.

           One thing only we ask:
           that we be kept in this knowing,
           and never fall away from this kind of life.

            When they had prayed, they embraced one another,
       and went to eat their holy meal, which had no blood in it.
