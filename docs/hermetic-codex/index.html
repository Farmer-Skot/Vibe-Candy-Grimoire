The Hermetic Codex

Transmissions from the Thrice-Great

Twelve pieces on the Hermetic teaching, from the Emerald Tablet to the sealing of the vessel. Each stands behind a gate.

  1. IThe Emerald TabletTThat which is above is like that which is below.
  2. IIThe Seven Hermetic PrinciplesRReality is a thought, and the thought keeps seven laws.
  3. IIIThe Three Pillars of WisdomIIn three arts one wisdom stands: the work, the stars, the gods.
  4. IVThe Great Work — Magnum OpusSStep into the black; the gold is on the far side of it.
  5. VThe Caduceus — Staff of HermesMMarry the two serpents, and the staff grows wings.
  6. VIThe Planes of ExistenceEEvery world is here; only your attention travels.
  7. VIIMental TransmutationGGo by degrees: a mood is a string to be tuned, not an enemy.
  8. VIIIThe Divine ParadoxIIt is a dream, and it is the only one. Hold both.
  9. IXInvocation of ThothSSpeak only what you would see written, and the Scribe answers.
  10. XThe Corpus HermeticumTThe one reading these words is what the words are about.
  11. XISolve et CoagulaUUnbind what has hardened; gather what has scattered; again.
  12. XIIThe Hermetic SealSSeal the vessel, keep the fire low, and open only to give.
